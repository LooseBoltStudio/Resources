# Contract Resources

Place contract agreements, amendments, memoranda, side letters, arbitration decisions, and related reference files in this folder.

## Browser readers

- `midsouth/` - BLET MidSouth Scheduled Rules Agreement
- `peb-250/` - CPKC-BLET agreement established pursuant to PEB 250
- `new-york-dock/` - New York Dock Article I, Section 4 implementing-agreement award

The New York Dock reader contains the January 16, 2026 arbitration award that establishes the implementing agreement for the CPKC-BLET consolidation. It applies the New York Dock protective conditions; it is not a copy of the complete 1979 conditions.
