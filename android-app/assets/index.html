Quick guide
Set your assignment from the Home card. Schedules refresh from GitHub and remain available offline. Orange matrix cells are assigned rest days. Open Files to search, zoom, bookmark, and save important PDF pages.
Sources and accuracy
Schedules come from the Loose Bolt Studio Resources repository and the listed assignment notices. Agreements and calendars are reference copies. Always verify against current carrier notices, agreements, and local instructions.
Privacy
The app has no account, advertising, or analytics. Favorites, bookmarks, assignment choices, and backups stay on your phone unless you choose to copy or share them. Internet access is used to check schedule and app updates.
